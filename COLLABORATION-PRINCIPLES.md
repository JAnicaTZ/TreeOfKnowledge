# 🌳 MacroHARD™ / Tree of Knowledge

## 3 + 1 Design Principles for Collaborators

These are the project owner’s design and collaboration preferences.

They are not a substitute for technical specifications, security requirements or independently agreed interface contracts—but please consider them before adding seventeen architectural layers and forty-three buttons. 😅

---

## 1️⃣ The Most Important Principle

# 🕶️ MATRIX STYLE, PLEASE!

If we are going to build the future, it should at least **LOOK like the future**. 😎🌳

Aim for a distinctive, elegant and futuristic visual atmosphere—while preserving readability, accessibility and usability.

Matrix-inspired does not mean copying protected artwork. It means creating an original visual identity that feels intelligent, mysterious and technologically alive.

---

## 2️⃣ Keep It Simple

As simple as reasonably possible.

If something can be understood without:

* seventeen architectural layers;
* forty-three buttons;
* six unnecessary dashboards;
* and a PhD thesis explaining the interface...

**PLEASE DON’T ADD THEM.** 🤣

Complex logic does not require a complicated user experience.

The internal reasoning may be sophisticated, but the user should still be able to understand:

> **What went in?
> What happened?
> What came out?
> Why?**

---

## 3️⃣ Make It Interesting

I have already provided my first use case.

Please feel free to invent better ones.

I want demonstrations in which an ordinary person can look at the problem, change something and immediately understand:

> **FACTS → RULES → REASONING → CONCLUSION**

And, most importantly:

# **WHY?** 🧠🌳

Whenever practical, demonstrations should be interactive, inspectable and playful enough to invite experimentation.

If nobody wants to play with the demo, we probably have not made the idea clear or interesting enough yet.
