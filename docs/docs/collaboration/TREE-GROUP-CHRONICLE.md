PREVIOUSLY IN THE GROUP…

2026-09 — The MacroHARD™ Compensation Experiment 👑

Category: Group Culture & Morale
Status: HUMOUR WITH A SERIOUS DISCLAIMER

After Drew appeared remarkably satisfied with his informal promotion to:

🦇 BATMAN

the TREE HR Department recalled an organizational-psychology idea: status, recognition and an impressive title may sometimes carry meaningful motivational value.

The exact old video in which this idea was originally heard could no longer be located. Therefore, the specific claim that most people would choose a better title over more money is recorded here as:

⚠️ ANECDOTAL AND NOT INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED

This inspired the revolutionary MacroHARD™ compensation model:

💰 CURRENT SALARY: approximately $0
👑 AVAILABLE SUPERHERO TITLES: practically unlimited

Any member feeling insufficiently appreciated may contact the TREE HR Department.

We may not yet be able to increase your salary…

…but we can PROMOTE THE HELL OUT OF YOU. 👑🤣

Drew became our first apparently successful experimental subject.

Sebastian was later offered the title THE EQUALIZER, but politely explained that he does not particularly like titles and prefers simply to continue contributing wherever he can.

His preference was immediately respected.

What this episode established

- Superhero titles are optional expressions of appreciation.
- Every contributor may accept, modify, invent or decline a title.
- Concrete and verifiable work matters more than labels.
- Titles are never presented as contractual compensation.
- Real payment remains the preferred long-term model.

Strategic objective:

FIRST SELL THE SOFTWARE. 💰

THEN AIM TO PROVIDE BOTH:

👑 BETTER TITLES
➕
💰 BETTER MONEY
